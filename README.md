1. Introduction
Welcome to Briefed, a Pomodoro-based focus application designed to help you manage your time effectively. Your privacy is a fundamental priority. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to your data security and transparency.

2. Data Collection
Briefed does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data. * No Personal Information: We do not ask for, or have access to, your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information.

No Usage Tracking: We do not track your location, your device ID, or how you interact with the app.

No Third-Party Analytics: There are no hidden "trackers" or third-party SDKs that gather information for advertising or profiling.

Any settings you adjust within the app (such as timer durations or notification preferences) are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to a server.

3. Data Sharing
Since we do not collect any data, we have no information to sell, trade, or share with third parties. Your focus sessions are entirely private to you.

4. Policy Updates
While we are committed to a "zero-data" philosophy, we may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.

User Advisory: You are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

5. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the "Briefed" philosophy, please feel free to reach out through the official support channels.
